The v1.1.0 system-state and the v1.1.2 report's What-Not-To-Do block were authored under the framing that lane-derivation infrastructure (Phase B of the lane-derivation directive) should be conditional on multi-user observational evidence that vocabulary widening (Phase A) was insufficient. The 2026-04-27 directive supersedes that framing on the following grounds:

**Preserved:**
- The six composing contracts (Report, SCL, HLMC, PAC, CASS, ARRSC) — all remain operative on every governed pass
- The terminal directive `docs/v1-1-0-completion-guidance.md` and its six v1.1.0 readiness conditions — all met; not contested
- The v1.0.x calibration anchor (10-persona harness) — remains the regression baseline; not contested
- v1.1.1 (persistence) and v1.1.2 (semantic bridge) closures — fully preserved
- The lane-derivation directive's Phase A vs. Phase B sequencing concept — preserved as a logical structure; the activation gate is what changes

**Rejected:**
- The "wait for multi-user evidence before Phase B" framing — the designer has determined from direct observation that the v1.1.2 system, while structurally correct, does not yet produce the actionable-path-feel that completion requires. Lane derivation and strategy compression are named as the deterministic responses; the directive treats further vocabulary widening as diminishing-returns.
- Treating "10-persona harness regression = pass" as load-bearing primary validation. Going forward, harness regression is necessary but not sufficient. The new completion criterion adds: lane alignment, cross-section coherence, final compression, and actionable-path-feel.

**Considered and consciously chosen:**
- **Considered:** running a second user-testing session on the v1.1.2 surface before activating Phase B (the v1.1.2 report's recommended next move).
- **Rejected:** the designer has determined that the system-level coherence problem (sections that don't talk to each other; output that lacks final compression) is observable directly without an N=2 trial, and the failure mode is structural, not vocabulary-bound.
- **Preserved:** the data point that v1.1.2 closes vocabulary-class fallback at the section level; this remains valid evidence and informs the design of v1.1.3 (lane derivation can build on, not replace, the v1.1.2 vocabulary substrate).

## Optimization Target (formalized)

> **A coherent, high-signal, actionable strategy that can be saved, revisited, and acted on.**

This replaces the prior implicit target ("structurally complete blueprint output across seven sections").

The shift is real:
- A structurally complete blueprint is necessary but no longer sufficient
- A coherent strategy must read as one connected path, not seven independent sections
- The output must compress into something the user can hold, revisit, and act on — not a long structured document they read once and abandon

---

## Completion Criterion (formalized)

> **"The user feels they have a clear, actionable path they can actually begin building."**

This is now the load-bearing evaluation question for every governed pass under this realignment. Section-level correctness, harness regression, and SCL compliance remain hard constraints, but they no longer answer the question on their own.

---

## Named Missing Pieces (per directive)

The directive identifies three structural gaps in the post-v1.1.2 system that the v1.1.3 + v1.1.4 sequence must close:

1. **Lane alignment** — the system reads inputs but does not yet route synthesis based on what kind of operating condition the user is in. Sections are computed independently against the same raw input, which produces internal incoherence when the input is abstract or compressed.
2. **Cross-section coherence** — the seven blueprint sections do not reinforce each other; a user reading the blueprint sees seven correct paragraphs that don't add up to one strategy.
3. **Final compression** — the blueprint ends without a synthesized "this is the path" output. The user is left to compress the blueprint themselves, which is what defeats the actionable-path-feel.

## Hard Stops (per directive)

The following are explicitly out of scope under this realignment:

- **Indefinite vocabulary expansion.** v1.1.2's 25 additions close the vocabulary widening lane. Further regex/canonical additions are diminishing-returns and are not authorized under this realignment unless a v1.1.3+ pass surfaces a specific bounded gap.
- **LLM introduction.** Pre-LLM intelligence (lane derivation + strategy compression) must be exhausted first. v1.2.0 or later, after the v1.1.3 + v1.1.4 evidence is in.
